# 10 · Open decisions and product flags

All decisions in this file are settled as of 1 Oct 2026 (IST); nothing here blocks implementation. Sushant
settled rows 1–10, 19 and 20 on 1 Oct. Rows 11–18 came out of the 30 Sep redesign and were settled the same day
with the standard answer; the boards on the design canvas already follow them. Rows 21–24 came out of writing the
Fee Market and Launch specs on 1 Oct and carry the standard answer until Sushant says otherwise. The boards were drawn before
1 Oct, so where a board differs from this file (the Senior target, Predict in SOL …), this file and
`11-CLICK-MAP.md` win.
Each decision has a dated line in `DECISIONS.md`. A new question gets a new row marked open, and its answer a
dated line in `DECISIONS.md`.

| 2 | **Is Predict legal where our users are** (settled 1 Oct) | A real-SOL Predict takes money on outcomes. India's online money-gaming ban is in force; in the US event contracts are a CFTC matter | Points only, no real SOL. Every signed-in wallet gets 100 points each epoch to call with (unused points do not carry over); a call puts 10, 25, 50 or 100 points on YES or NO; when the market resolves from the final Fee Index value, the losing side's points are shared among the winners in proportion to their calls (no fee); the leaderboard ranks net points won over the last 30 epochs; points have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or transferred. A call needs the sign-in session but no wallet transaction (`POST /v1/predict/calls`, request #11). The Panta / real-SOL path stays behind the flag `PREDICT_REAL_SOL` (off) until there is legal advice. Spec: `pages/predict.md` |
| 3 | **Which markets Panta can run** (settled 1 Oct) | `docs/SIDE_TRACKS.md` plans one Panta market per epoch on the Fee Index and still asks whether Panta can resolve from our index; Panta's fee is unknown | The Fee Index market only, in points: `api_app` serves it and resolves it from the final Fee Index value, and Panta is not used while `PREDICT_REAL_SOL` is off. Points mode has no fee, so the fee placeholder is gone. The other three markets stay on the design canvas only |
| 4 | **Credit limits look small for mid-size validators** (settled 1 Oct) | NTT DOCOMO GLOBAL (193,097 SOL, 5% fee) gets ≈ 4.7 SOL (7.6 hedged) from 25%/40% of 10 epochs of swept revenue | Rates stay. Next to every credit limit the app shows what it covers, from data it already has: "covers ≈ N epochs of vote fees (≈ $X)", where N = limit ÷ `network.voteFeesPerEpochSol` (1 decimal) and X = limit × `network.price.solUsd` (whole dollars). NTT DOCOMO on the 29 Sep data (2.16 SOL of vote fees an epoch, $119.80 a SOL): 4.7 SOL covers ≈ 2.2 epochs (≈ $563); 7.6 hedged covers ≈ 3.5 epochs (≈ $910). Rows VP22, VP37, VP40, VP42 and TE12; `pages/validator.md` 4d |

| 21 | **Fee Market v1: who quotes, who trades, where** (settled 1 Oct, standard answer) | ADR 0004: v1 trades against a seeded maker, no order book. The program allows one swap per quote per wallet (`["swap", quote, taker]`). The hedged flag follows plan F7: Receive-fixed swaps on each of the next 5 epochs, each ≥ 50% of average revenue, which moves the limit from 25% to 40%. Spec: `pages/fee-market.md` |

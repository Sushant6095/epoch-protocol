# 11 · Click map: what every click does

As of 1 Oct 2026 (IST). One row per interactive element on every page, in page order: 322 rows (the Fee Market and
Launch rows, FM and LP, were added on 1 Oct). Each row says
what the element is, where it sits, what triggers it, what happens in the real app (route and URL params, UI change),
which hook, endpoint, Epoch instruction or wallet call it uses, and its loading, empty, error and success states.

**How to use it.** Build a page with its board open on the design canvas (press Play: every link, tab, button and flow
works) and this file beside it. Treat every row as a test case: when a page is done, each of its rows should pass in
Playwright. If the real app needs something a row doesn't cover, add a row, never guess.

**Row prefixes:** SH app shell · LA landing · TE terminal · VE validators explorer · VP validator profile + Manage ·
MS My Stake (sign-in and Predict included) · VA vault · FM Fee Market (a Terminal tab) · LP launch. Canvas boards: `Main`,
`Terminal`, `Validators`, `Validator`, `MyStake`, `Vault`; the Fee Market and Launch have no board (added after the
canvas): build them from their Refero screens and the rows below.

**Prototype-only controls (do not build):** the "Viewing as: Public · Operator (demo)" switch and the demo state switch on
the validator profile; the dashed "Prototype" rows in the wallet steps on My Stake (wallet approves / rejects / Devnet);
"Skip ahead 3 epochs (demo)" and "Start the demo over". They exist so every state can be reached on the canvas.

**Words.** Advance, borrow, sweep, bond, tranche (Senior, Junior), target (never "guaranteed"), share price, Fee Index.
Predict: call, market, pool, payout, points (never bet, gamble, wager, odds). Fee Market: swap, fixed rate, notional,
collateral, hedge, settle, Pay fixed, Receive fixed (never bet, gamble, wager, odds, long, short). Launch: revenue token,
share, term, curve, raise, graduate, buyback, burn, backing (never investment, dividend, guaranteed, APY or profit).
A missing value is "—", never 0.

## Vault · `/vault` · board `Vault.dc.html`

Public to read; a wallet to deposit or withdraw. Data: `useVault()` (`GET /v1/vault` + WS `vault`), `useNetwork()` (epoch, slot, countdown, median APY), `useSession()` (wallet, roles), `useLenderPosition(address)` (the wallet's two Lender PDAs, seeds `lender`, pool, owner, tranche, plus its open WithdrawRequest accounts; request #8d), wallet balance from RPC `getBalance`. Transactions are built with epoch-sdk (request #9): `deposit`, `request_withdraw`, `cancel_withdraw` (the last one is request #9b); the queue is paid by the `process_withdrawal` crank. Every rule on the page (0.03%, 10%, 20%, 60%, 10 epochs, 5,000 SOL cap) is read from `VaultSnapshot.params`, never typed in. The Vault uses the Epoch program, which runs on devnet for now (decision 6): a "Devnet" badge by the title and the line "Epoch's program runs on devnet for now. Switch your wallet to devnet to sign."; its Sample badges go once the devnet Pool exists. URL state: `?tranche=senior|junior&action=deposit|withdraw&amount=&view=price|yield|util&table=loans|queue|lenders|params&filter=&q=`.

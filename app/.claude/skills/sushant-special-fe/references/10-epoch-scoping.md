# 10 — Epoch scoping (which surface gets what)

Epoch has two definitions of "premium", and the layout of both comes from Refero (`handover/12-REPLICA-BLUEPRINTS.md`).

## Landing `/` → this skill, inside the Kraken staking-landing replica
- Keep Kraken's structure: marketing bar, two-column hero with a number-led headline and a 16:9 media box,
  proof band, three numbered steps, a 4-column card grid, two-column FAQ, disclaimer box, full-bleed footer.
- Premium lives in the details: the epoch-ring hero object (frame sequence, R3F or shadergradient; the only
  WebGL on the site), SplitText headline, number tickers, staggered reveals, Lenis smooth scroll.
- Components come from registries (Magic UI, Aceternity, Animate UI, Motion Primitives, React Bits, 21st.dev),
  re-themed to `--ep-*` and logged in `handover/PROVENANCE.md`.

## App pages → not this skill
Terminal, Validators, Validator profile, My Stake, Predict and Vault follow their Refero replicas: Mercury
shell, dense tables, tabular numbers, calm dark tokens, micro-motion only (150–250 ms, transform and opacity).
No neumorphism, glass, grain or decorative 3D next to live data.

## Sign in → light touch
The Reown split: a soft gradient from Epoch tokens on the left panel is the only decoration.
